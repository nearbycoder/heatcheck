The game

Arcade rules. No refs.
All heat.

HEAT CHECK is an original, over-the-top 2-on-2 arcade basketball game in the spirit of the 90s cabinets. Four quarters of turbo, ten-foot-high dunks and legal shoving. Every model, texture, animation and sound in it was generated from code.

Posterized!

Hold turbo, drive the lane and press Shoot inside dunk range. The announcer, the crowd and the backboard all let the other team know about it.

  • Hold to burn

    Turbo

    Sprint past defenders, chase down breaks and power up every move. Watch the stamina bar under your name.

  • Turbo + Shoot

    Dunks

    Distance picks the style: two-hand, windmill, 360, reverse, and a flip when you’re on fire.

  • Turbo + Pass

    Alley-oops

    Lob it to a teammate cutting to the rim and they finish it above the cylinder.

  • No fouls. Ever.

    Shoving

    Push a ball handler back to jar it loose, or clear space for your teammate. Turbo + Shove knocks them flat.

  • 1Bucket 2Heating up 3On fire
    2 in a row → 3 in a row

    Heating up → On fire

    Make two straight without the other team scoring and you’re heating up. Make three and you’re ON FIRE: unlimited turbo, deadly shooting, longer dunk range, harder shoves and steals, a flame trail on the ball and a burning net. You can even goaltend. It lasts until they score or you hit four more.

  • Shoot on defense

    Blocks

    Time your jump and send it into the third row. Swat it on the way down, though, and it’s goaltending.

  • Rookie · Pro · Legend

    CPU catch-up

    Just like the old cabinets, the CPU finds a little extra when it falls far behind. How much depends on difficulty.

  • Option menu

    Big head mode

    Exactly what it sounds like. Flip it on before tip-off.

  • Same keyboard or pads

    2 players

    Team up in co-op against the CPU, or go head-to-head with a CPU teammate each. Or just watch CPU vs CPU.

How to play

Grab a pad.
Find your release.

Pick a mode, choose both teams, and the referee tosses the jump ball. Press Shoot after the release to tip it to your teammate.

The browser version supports keyboard and gamepad. Plug in a controller and press any button so your browser can see it. Two players can share one keyboard, or use a pad each.

The rules

Only two whistles
in the building.

The classic arcade rules, re-created.

  1. 01

    2-on-2, four quarters

    Quarters run 1 to 5 minutes (3 by default), with overtime if it’s tied. A jump ball starts the game and each overtime. Quarters 2 and 3 start with the tip loser inbounding, quarter 4 with the tip winner.

  2. 02

    No fouls

    Shoving, elbowing and knocking people down are all legal.

  3. 03

    Two violations

    The 24-second shot clock and goaltending. Block a shot on its way down and the basket counts.

  4. 04

    Heating up & on fire

    Two in a row without the other team scoring: heating up (+accuracy). Three: ON FIRE.

    • Unlimited turbo, much better shooting, more dunk range, harder shoves and steals
    • Flame trail on the ball, and the net burns when you score
    • Goaltend without penalty
    • Goes out when the other team scores, or after 4 more baskets

  5. 05

    Inbounds

    After a made basket the other team inbounds from under that basket. The clock stops during inbounds.

  6. 06

    Threes & buzzer-beaters

    3-pointers count from beyond the arc or in the corners. Buzzer-beaters count.

Modes & options

  • 1 Player vs CPU You plus a CPU teammate.
  • 2 Players co-op Both humans on the same team.
  • 2 Players head-to-head Each human gets a CPU teammate.
  • CPU vs CPU Sit back and watch the AI play.
  • Difficulty Rookie, Pro or Legend.
  • Big head mode On or off. (On.)

Built from code

Zero hand‑made assets.
Everything is a script.

There are no hand-made assets in HEAT CHECK. Python scripts in Blender build every model, rig, animation, texture and logo. Another script synthesizes every sound effect, crowd bed, announcer line and music loop. The desktop game is C++ on Unreal Engine 5.9, with no Blueprints.

  1. Step 1

    Blender Python

    Players, a 25-bone rig, 22 animation clips, the court, hoops, ball, arena, crowd, logos and badges.

    Blender/gen_*.py

  2. Step 2

    Export

    FBX meshes and animations, PNG textures, synthesized WAV audio and JSON material manifests.

    Art/Export/

  3. Desktop

    Unreal Engine 5.9 C++

    Import scripts build the materials and map. Gameplay, AI, ball physics, cameras, HUD and audio are all C++.

    Source/HeatCheck/

    Browser

    Three.js web port

    The same Blender-generated models, animations and synthesized audio, running in your browser.
